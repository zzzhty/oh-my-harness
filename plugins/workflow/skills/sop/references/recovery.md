# SOP Recovery And Continuation (Conditional)

Read this reference only when a SOP execution has a failed validation or an interruption for which authorized diagnosis, repair, or continuation is possible. It supplements the owning SOP's actual Stop Conditions; it does not grant recovery, retries, permissions, or a fresh admission if the procedure explicitly terminates.

Honor explicit stop conditions, including a user's stop, safety or permission boundaries, immediately. On other required-validation failures, pause dependent steps and preserve failure and diagnostic evidence. Within current authorization, follow the SOP's declared diagnosis, repair or bounded recovery; keep report-only work non-mutating. Keep the original acceptance criteria. Reuse passing evidence only where the original owner and its verifier accept that it still covers the current tested state under its exact-source, dependency, configuration, environment and acceptance bindings. Reassess affected coverage after relevant changes or later failures; earlier passes cannot override later failures. Revalidate failed checks and repair-affected coverage, and establish required state prerequisites before continuing unfinished steps whose dependencies are satisfied. Independent authorized work may continue.

Follow owner-required fresh/full execution, explicit termination and complete transaction/rollback rules. New attempts must meet the original owner's applicable requirements for fresh admission, reestablishing fixtures, identities, resources and leases, and completing operations, final readback and cleanup in order. Do not splice old mutable state into qualification or hot-edit running candidates. A session, namespace or phase change alone neither invalidates passing evidence nor resets retry budgets. Without a repair, relevant input change or new diagnosis, do not repeat the same failure blindly. If recovery is unavailable, exhausted or needs additional authorization, stop the affected operation and report the evidence and needed input.
